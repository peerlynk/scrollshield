## Full Description
Protect your attention and stop scrolling on autopilot.

ScrollShield is an Android digital-wellbeing tool designed to help you notice when intentional feed usage turns into continuous, automatic scrolling.

### Key Features
- **Protected Apps Selection**: Choose which feed-heavy apps (Instagram, YouTube Shorts, X, Snapchat) you want ScrollShield to monitor.
- **Forward Scroll Gesture Counting**: Accurately counts forward swipe gestures on device without reading message text or post content.
- **Progressive Milestone Check-Ins**: Brings up calm perspective prompts at milestone counts (10, 25, 40, 50, 75, 100) to help you choose what happens next.
- **Adaptive Guardian Styles**: Customize reminder prompts to reflect your study, career, family presence, or physical health goals.
- **Floating Counter Overlay**: Optional floating 🛡️ badge displaying your live scroll count over protected feeds.
- **Native Android Home Widget**: View daily scroll totals, protected time, and protection status right from your home screen.
- **100% Local Privacy**: No account required, zero network telemetry, no screen recording, and no data uploaded to servers.
