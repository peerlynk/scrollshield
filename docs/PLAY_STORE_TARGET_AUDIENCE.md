## Target Audience Declaration
- **Primary Audience**: Users aged 13 and older seeking digital wellbeing and attention awareness tools for mobile social media feeds.
- **Child Directed**: ScrollShield is **NOT** designed specifically for children under 13 years of age.
- **Age Personalization**: ScrollShield provides user-selectable reminder styles (e.g. Study/Skills for students, Career/Projects for professionals). All personalization logic runs locally on-device without psychological profiling or age verification data collection.
- **Optional Higher-Intensity Modes**: Age-aware opt-in mechanisms ensure that optional higher-intensity reminder styles require explicit user enablement.
