## Content Questionnaire Answers (for reference during Play Console submission)

3. **Social / Communication Features**: None. ScrollShield contains no chat rooms, messaging engines, or social sharing surfaces.
4. **Location Sharing**: None. ScrollShield does not access or share GPS/location data.
5. **In-App Purchases / Digital Goods**: None.
6. **Advertisements**: None. ScrollShield contains no advertising SDKs.
